Acknowledgement of Country

HireHub recognises the historical and cultural significance of the Kulin Nation, the Eora Nation and the Noongar Nation, on which our Melbourne, Sydney and Perth offices are located. Each day we all walk on traditional Indigenous lands. We therefore acknowledge the traditional custodians of each of these lands, namely the Wurundjeri Woi Wurrung people, the Gadigal people and the Whadjuk people, and pay respect to Elders past and present.

As a travel technology platform, HireHub is committed to partnering with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander-led tourism operators, ensuring cultural experiences on our platform are curated in genuine consultation with the communities they represent.
